The artist

          
The hands behind the shine

          
Liza — around the lot as @hallyscomet — started bedazzling frames for friends before shows and accidentally built a small empire of glitter. What began as one leopard-print pair is now a rotating collection of one-of-one spectacles, shades and goggles.

          
No factories, no catalogs, no repeats. Just a bench, a tray of stones, and the unshakeable belief that Tuesday is a perfectly good day to look like the encore.
