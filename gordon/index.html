The Custom Ritual

Send me your frames, let's talk

Grandma's old rims, festival souvenirs, the pair you already love — upcycle them into the loudest thing in your bag. Four steps, zero boring pairs.

1.

Slide into the DMs

Screenshot a design you love, or bring your own frames. DM @sparktacles — yes, a real human reads it.

2.

Talk vision

Liza sketches the concept, swaps gem colors with you, and quotes your one-of-one. No two pairs, ever.

3.

The bedazzling

Every stone is placed by hand, sealed, and inspected twice. Sparks fly. Neighbors ask questions.

4.

Shine on

Your specs arrive in a Sparktacles pouch, ready for the lot, the slopes, or the grocery store.

No cart. No database. Just a good old-fashioned DM — the way custom work should be.

The Artist

The hands behind the shine

Liza — also known around the lot as @hallyscomet — started bedazzling frames for friends before shows and accidentally built a small empire of glitter. What began as one pair of leopard-print sunnies is now a rotating collection of one-of-one spectacles, shades and goggles.

Every stone is set by hand at her workbench, usually with a setlist playing. If you catch the booth at a market, bring your frames — she'll talk gems with you for as long as the line allows.
