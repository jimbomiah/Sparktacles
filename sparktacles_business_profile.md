# Business Profile: Sparktacles

Based on the provided images, "Screenshot 2026-10-24 095515.jpg", "Screenshot 2026-10-24 095557.jpg", "sparktaclesicon.jpg", and "sparktacleslogo.jpg", here is a comprehensive overview of the business **Sparktacles**.

## Overview

**Sparktacles** is a boutique, custom eyewear business run by an artist named Liza. Operating primarily through Instagram, the business specializes in hand-decorated, bedazzled, and highly expressive glasses and sunglasses.

The brand's tagline perfectly encapsulates its mission: *"Let your spectacles sparkle!"*

## Products and Offerings

Sparktacles focuses on applying rhinestones, gems, and colorful embellishments to various types of eyewear. Their product range includes:

* **Everyday Spectacles:** Custom-jeweled rims for standard prescription glasses.
* **Statement Sunglasses:** Oversized, brightly colored sunglasses featuring intricate rhinestone patterns (e.g., leopard print, solid gemstone borders, multi-colored rainbow designs).
* **Novelty Frames:** Including "third-eye" sunglasses (featuring a middle lens on the forehead) and specialized shapes.
* **Ski Goggles:** Large winter sports goggles heavily adorned with gems and unique designs like eyes.

## Business Model & Purchasing

Sparktacles utilizes a direct-to-consumer, conversational commerce model:

* **Direct Messaging (DM):** Customers are encouraged to send a DM if they see a ready-made design they like.
* **Custom Orders:** A core pillar of the business is custom work. Liza encourages customers to *"send me your frames, let's talk,"* allowing patrons to upcycle or personalize their own favorite eyewear.
* **In-Person Vending:** The business frequently sets up booths at vendor markets. Flyers for **PhanArt** events strongly indicate a presence at niche, community-driven craft fairs.

## Target Audience and Brand Vibe

The visual aesthetic of Sparktacles is vibrant, joyful, and deeply connected to the live music and festival community.

* **The Phish/Jam Band Community:** The repeated presence of "PhanArt" flyers strongly indicates that Sparktacles caters heavily to the Phish fan community and the broader jam band/music festival scene, where expressive, colorful, and wearable art is highly valued.
* **Customer Base:** The photos show a diverse, smiling customer base of all ages wearing the glasses at sunny outdoor venues, concerts, and social gatherings. The vibe is whimsical, sparkly, and highly personal.
