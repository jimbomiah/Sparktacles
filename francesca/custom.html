The process

Four steps, zero mystery

Say hi in the DMs

Tell me what you have got — frames, colors, patterns you love. A photo of your pair helps me plan the layout.

Lock the design

We pick stones, colors and coverage together, and you get a quote before any gem is touched.

Ship or drop your frames

Once the design is locked, send your frames over. Prescription lenses stay exactly as they are — gems live on the frames.

Sparkle and return

Most pairs are back on your face in one to two weeks, set and sealed, packed in a case.

Answers

Asked at every show

How long does a custom pair take?

Most pairs are back on your face in one to two weeks after we lock the design. Goggles and heavy-coverage pieces can take longer — I will give you a real timeline before we start.

Do you bedazzle prescription glasses?

All the time. The gems go on the frames only, never the lenses, so your prescription stays exactly as your optometrist cut it.

I do not have frames yet — can you supply them?

Yes. Ready-made pairs live in the gallery, and I can source base frames for custom work. DM me and we will talk options.

How do I care for a Sparktacles pair?

Treat them like the jewelry they are: store them in their case, wipe lenses with a soft cloth, and keep them away from chlorine and steam. Every stone is sealed, but rhinestones and hot tubs do not mix.

How does payment work?

Custom commissions are half up front and half when your pair is finished. Ready-made pairs are first come, first served in the DMs.
