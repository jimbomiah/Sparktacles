The commission ritual

From your frames to the floor

Have frames you adore but refuse to blend in? Ship them over. Four steps between here and your unveiling, with a real human at every step.

  1. 01

    The petition

    DM @sparktacles with what you're dreaming of — colors, patterns, chaos level. Liza draws it up and quotes it.

  2. 02

    The dispatch

    Box up your frames and ship them in. No frames? No problem — she sources quality pairs to sparkle on.

  3. 03

    In the workshop

    Every stone placed by hand, sealed, double-checked under lights. Usually with a setlist playing.

  4. 04

    The unveiling

    They arrive in a Sparktacles pouch, one of one, ready for the lot, the slopes, or Tuesday.

The curator

            
One artist, one table,
a lot of tiny stones

            
Liza — known on the lot as @hallyscomet — started bejeweling her own frames for shows and accidentally built an archive. What began as one pair of leopard-print sunnies is now a rotating collection of one-of-one spectacles, shades and goggles.

            
Every stone is set by hand at her workbench, usually with a setlist playing. Catch the booth at a market and she’ll talk gems with you for as long as the line allows. She also vends with PhanArt across the lot scene.
