Meet the sparkle dealer

Hi, I’m Liza — artist, gem collector, and the hands behind every single rhinestone. What started as bejeweling my own glasses for shows turned into Sparktacles: wearable art for people who like their eyewear loud, joyful, and impossible to lose in a crowd.

Every piece is one-of-one, hand-placed gem by gem, and regularly spotted glowing in festival crowds — especially out on PhanArt lots, where the shoes are comfortable and the glasses are not.

the custom ritual

Send me your frames

Got a beloved pair of glasses that deserves more glitter? Mail them over — they come back transformed. Here’s how the sparkle happens:

  1. 1

    Slide into my DMs

    Tell me your wildest eyewear dream — or your exact vision. We talk colors, gems, and vibes until it feels right.

  2. 2

    Send me your frames

    Box up your favorite specs, sunnies, or goggles. Don’t have a pair? I’ll source quality frames for you.

  3. 3

    Sparkle magic

    Every rhinestone is placed by hand, sealed with care, and shipped back ready for its stage debut.
